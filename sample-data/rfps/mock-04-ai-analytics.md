# Mock RFP 04: AI and Analytics

## Account

Bank 4

## Purpose

This synthetic RFP is provided only for endpoint validation and hackathon demonstrations. It contains no customer-confidential information.

## Mandatory requirements

1. Design a governed analytics and machine-learning platform.
2. Define model monitoring, explainability, and human review.
3. Return a traceable requirements matrix with source references.
4. Identify evidence gaps, clarification questions, owners, dependencies, and risks.

## Evaluation criteria

- Completeness and traceability
- Feasibility and delivery governance

- Relevant approved evidence
- Commercial transparency

## Commercial question

Identify platform, model, and data-engineering cost drivers.
